How the simulation was run

  • Each weekday has its own account. Monday trades never mix with Tuesday trades. A blown account is replaced on the next trade for that weekday.
  • Targets, the daily loss, the maximum drawdown, and the fee depend on the account type. Reaching either loss line blows the account. One fee covers every phase of that challenge. Each phase, and the funded account, starts again at $5,000.
  • A payout needs at least $20 of closed profit and 10 Monday–Friday days after the first funded trade of that cycle. You keep 80%. The balance then returns to $5,000. The 2-step account also needs 3 new days of at least $25. The 1-step and 3-step accounts have no minimum number of days.
  • The sl50 fade run is 0.10 lot, $2 a point. The sl100 continuation runs are 0.09 lot, $1.80 a point. The fade run sells a cross up and buys a cross down. The continuation runs buy a cross up and sell a cross down. Touch means a wick through the level is enough. Prices are the bid series, with no spread.
  • A new phase starts only when the account is flat. If last week's trade is still open when the same weekday signals again, that position stays on the same account until it closes.
