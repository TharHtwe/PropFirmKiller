Three runs on the 2-step account

Standard 2-step, $5,000, fee $22. One account for each New York weekday. Reaching $4,600, or that day's 4% line, blows the account. You keep 80% of funded profit. Cash is settled on 26 Oct 2026. Trades run from 2020 through 23 Oct 2026.

Net cash is payouts you received minus fees. Raw dollars are the points times the lot, before blowups and the split. A blown challenge costs the fee. The firm's $5,000 absorbs the trading loss.

How this was run
  • Each weekday has its own account. Monday trades never mix with Tuesday trades. A blown account is replaced on the next trade for that weekday. No new account is bought after the last trade.
  • Phase 1 needs $400 and 3 days of at least $25. Phase 2 needs $250 and 3 new days of at least $25. Each phase, and the funded account, starts again at $5,000. Challenge profit is not paid. One $22 fee covers both phases.
  • Reaching $4,600, or that day's 4% equity floor, blows the account. The daily floor resets at 00:00 UTC from the higher of equity and balance, including an open trade.
  • A payout withdraws all closed profit above $5,000. It needs at least $20 of profit, 3 winning days, and 10 Monday–Friday days after the first funded trade of that cycle. The next clock starts on the first trade after the payout. You keep 80%. The balance returns to $5,000.
  • The fade run sells a cross up and buys a cross down, 0.10 lot, $2 a point. The continuation runs buy a cross up and sell a cross down, 0.05 lot, $1 a point. Touch means a wick through the level is enough. Prices are the bid series, with no spread and no commission.
  • The fade trades are the stored combined list, split by the New York entry date. A live separate account could also take a signal that list skipped while another day's trade was open.
