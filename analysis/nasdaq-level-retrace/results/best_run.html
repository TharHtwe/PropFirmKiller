Best run, trades taken

sl50_all_every_one_month. One trade on a New York date. The first level crossed while London or New York is open. Stop 50 points, target 50, levels from the previous 30 days. Skipped touches are not listed.

Times are UTC, so they match a TradingView chart set to UTC. The date, weekday, week, and month use the New York calendar date of that entry. London is 08:00–16:30 London time. New York is 08:00–17:00 New York time. RTH is 09:30–16:00 New York time. On 23 October 2026 those are 07:00–15:30 UTC, 12:00–21:00 UTC, and 13:30–20:00 UTC. A trade can sit in more than one of those windows. Choosing a session keeps the trades whose entry bar was inside it. All keeps every taken trade.

The 1st week is days 1–7 of the month, then 8–14, 15–21, and 22–28. Days 29–31 are listed on their own so they are not folded into the 4th week. Weeks are pooled across months. January is every January in the sample, pooled the same way, and the same for the other months.
